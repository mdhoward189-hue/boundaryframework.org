Mike Howard directs the Murray G. Bacon Center for AI Ethics in Business at Iowa State University's Ivy College of Business, where he is the Hilsinger-Janson Professor of Management and Entrepreneurship. The center is a home for research, student learning and industry engagement on the responsible use of AI in organizations: where it creates value, and where human judgment still has to lead. The Boundary Framework grew out of that work.

Mike came to research from industry. He trained as an aeronautical engineer at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign and managed automotive manufacturing projects as a project engineer. After an MBA in finance from Illinois, he spent seven years at Intel, where he managed product cost for microprocessors and supported long-range planning for the processor roadmap.

He earned his PhD in business administration from the University of Washington's Foster School of Business in 2012, then taught at Texas A&M University for ten years as an assistant and then associate professor of management. He joined Iowa State in 2022 and was named director of the Bacon Center when it launched in March 2026.

His research focuses on innovation and entrepreneurship, with an emphasis on social network analysis. A recent study in the Strategic Management Journal, with Emily Cox-Pahnke and Tiona Zuzul, shows how new medical device firms can speed regulatory clearance through how they position their products. Above all, he works to help students of every background reach their full potential in business, scholarship or whatever field they choose.
